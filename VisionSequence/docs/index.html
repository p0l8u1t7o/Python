VisionSequence documentation

Everything under docs/ is plain HTML with its screenshots in docs/img/: each page carries its own styling, depends on nothing external, and opens straight in a browser or from the product at /docs/<page>.html. The global AI assistant answers from these pages and links to the section it used. Development conventions, the verification checklist and the Git rules live in CLAUDE.md at the project root; README.md is the quick start.

Integrating and operating

DocumentWhat it covers
AutomationBeing called: HTTP synchronous runs, one-line TCP commands, live SSE events, pushing images, performance advice, identity and the engine lock, the integration pages — and the quality-data endpoints an MES can read (SPC series and alerts, precision studies) plus the OCR font, shape model and statistical template assets.
ModbusCalling out and being polled: connection kinds (Modbus TCP client, Modbus server, TCP client, TCP image push, plugins) and their config, address format, the write and read mapping tables, triggering a flow from a device flag, degradation, the connection API and writing your own Writer.
Capture clientWhen the camera is on another PC or needs a vendor SDK: a downloadable desktop program drives webcams, Basler and IDS cameras where they are and connects out to the server's capture port. Shared memory on the same machine, lossless TCP across machines; channels, ROI, parameters, on-demand grabs and continuous streaming; the "capture client camera" source, protocol v1 and the acceptance checklist.
Deployment and operationsPutting the platform on a customer's PC and keeping it running: the setup wizard and the install root, the vsctl command line, Windows services, ports and firewall, HTTPS and the client PCs that only run a browser, accounts and keys, backup and restore, upgrading and rolling back, plugins and the deep-learning pack on site, monitoring, several stations, disaster recovery, security, and building a release.

Extending

DocumentWhat it covers
PluginsDrop a file in a folder and it loads: subclass Tool, Grabber, Writer or Trainer, put it in plugins/ and it is detected automatically. Module-level variables control the display name, description and whether it loads at all; the Plugins page shows what mounted and why something failed; two shipped examples and the rules for writing your own.
Front-end / back-end contractThe graph JSON, ROI regions, overlays, the RunReport, the advanced preview endpoints and the front-end skeleton — what a parallel work stream needs to agree on.

DocumentWhat it covers
Workflow designThe thinking behind the platform: why an in-process dataflow DAG rather than a control-flow engine, closed sets and safety boundaries, keeping the database off the hot path, degradation strategy, and the extension seams.
ArchitectureHow the platform is built: data model, tool framework, graph validation and compilation, the engine, Runner concurrency, image cache, image sources, HTTP/SSE/TCP, the front end, accounts and the engine lock, settings, tests and the pitfalls we hit.
PerformanceMeasured figures for the engine and the tools, the record of what each optimisation bought, the delivery measurements of every new tool, the honest GPU pre-processing verdict, and the precision studies (repeatability, reproducibility, gauge R&R) with real reports.
